# Scenic Luxury Cruises & Tours and Emerald Cruises — Brand Research

Prepared for a Dubai-based B2B travel site. Presented together as "Scenic & Emerald Cruises" — sister brands under the same corporate umbrella (see Section 3 for the officially confirmed detail on this relationship).

STRICT SOURCE POLICY applied: only official Scenic (scenicusa.com / scenic.cruises / scenic.eu / scenic.com.au / scenic.co.uk) and official Emerald Cruises (emeraldcruises.com / emerald.cruises / emeraldcruises.eu / emeraldcruises.co.uk / emeraldcruises.com.au) domains were used. No trade press, blogs or Wikipedia were used as sources. Anything not confirmable on the official sites is marked **UNVERIFIED**. All fares/prices seen on official pages have been deliberately omitted per brief; no specific departure dates are given, only representative durations and season months. British spelling used throughout.

### Scenic Eclipse — discovery yachts (ocean)

Two sister "Discovery Yachts" — Scenic Eclipse (launched 2019) and Scenic Eclipse II — market themselves as "The World's First Discovery Yacht™." Specifications: 168 metres long, 17,545 GT, Polar Class 6 (1A-Super per Scenic's own itinerary pages), up to 228 guests (reduced to c. 200 in Antarctica), 114 suites (344 sq ft Verandah Suites up to the 2,658 sq ft Two-Bedroom Owner's Penthouse Suite), roughly 1:1 crew-to-guest ratio. Onboard: two Airbus H130-T2 helicopters (six guests + pilot), the custom-built Scenic Neptune submersible (rated to 100 m), Senses Spa with salt-therapy lounge, Sky Deck vitality pool, specialty dining (Koko's Asian Fusion, Lumière), kayaks and paddleboards. (Source: Scenic official — https://scenic.cruises/US/en-US/ocean/ocean-experience/ship-experience/scenic-eclipse)

Scenic's About Us page also references a third ocean ship, "Eclipse III"/"Ikon," slated to launch in 2028, alongside Eclipse and Eclipse II. **UNVERIFIED** in detail — the page names it in passing as part of fleet growth but no full specification page was found. (Source: Scenic official — https://scenic.cruises/US/en-US/about-us)

## 3. Positioning & Clientele

**Scenic** describes itself (About Us) as established in 1986, operating in "over 100 countries and every continent," and states it "set[s] the benchmark in truly all-inclusive, ultra-luxury travel." It cites industry recognition, including Scenic Eclipse being named "Best Cruise Ship for Dining," "Asia's Best River Cruise Ship" and "Europe's Best River Cruise Line" at the 2022 World Cruise Awards. Scenic Group is referenced as the parent entity, including an in-house shipbuilding arm named "MKM Yachts." (Source: Scenic official — https://scenic.cruises/US/en-US/about-us)

Scenic's river product is positioned as all-inclusive ultra-luxury with intimate ship sizes (as low as 68–163 guests), all-weather Sun Lounges, multiple dining venues and high space ratios ("Space-Ships"). Scenic Eclipse is positioned as a "Discovery Yacht" — a hybrid of superyacht-style luxury and genuine polar/expedition capability (Polar Class 6/1A-Super, helicopters, submersible), aimed at destination-immersive, small-ship ocean/expedition travel rather than mainstream cruising. (Source: Scenic official — https://scenic.cruises/US/en-US/ocean/ocean-experience/ship-experience/scenic-eclipse)

**Emerald Cruises** describes itself (About Us) as "an award-winning travel operator specialising in deluxe river cruises and luxury yacht cruises," founded in 2013 with first sailings in 2014. Its river product emphasises all-inclusive comprehensive comfort (contemporary suites, elegant dining venues, spacious Sun Decks) at a positioning generally understood as accessible deluxe rather than the ultra-luxury tier; its yacht product (Azzurra/Sakara/Kaia) is styled as boutique "yacht cruising" — access to small ports larger ships cannot reach, an included-excursion-every-day "EmeraldDISCOVERY" programme, Marina Platform water toys, and all-inclusive pricing covering port charges, meals with beverages, Wi-Fi and gratuities. (Source: Emerald official — https://emerald.cruises/US/en-US/about-us; https://emerald.cruises/US/en-US/yacht/destinations/mediterranean-adriatic-sea)

**Confirmed sister-brand relationship**: Emerald Cruises' official site footer states "The distribution of the cruises and tours on this website is managed by Scenic Tours (USA), Inc.," and the site references a shared "Scenic and Emerald Rewards" loyalty programme and shared "Scenic Group" sustainability initiatives — confirming Emerald sits within the same corporate structure as Scenic (Scenic Group), i.e. they are officially confirmed sister brands, even though Scenic's own About Us page does not explicitly name Emerald. (Source: Emerald official — https://emerald.cruises/US/en-US/about-us)

## 4. Notes for GCC Agents

- **Positioning split for client conversations**: Scenic river (Space-Ships) and Scenic Eclipse sit at the ultra-luxury end (smaller guest counts, higher space ratios, all-inclusive with premium extras like the Salt Therapy Room, Culinaire cooking school, and — on Eclipse — helicopters/submersible). Emerald river (Star-Ships) and Emerald yachts (Azzurra/Sakara/Kaia) sit at a more accessible deluxe/boutique-yacht tier under the same parent group — useful for offering clients a "good–better" pairing within one relationship/one loyalty programme (Scenic and Emerald Rewards).
- **Red Sea and Seychelles/Indian Ocean yacht seasons** (Emerald Azzurra/Sakara) are geographically the most relevant regional product for Dubai-based clients — embarkation ports include Aqaba, Jeddah, Cairo and Athens (Red Sea/Suez itineraries) and Mahé/Mombasa/Nairobi (Seychelles & Indian Ocean, with East Africa safari add-ons). These are worth leading with for GCC-origin guests given proximity and flight time.
- **Mekong river product** (Scenic Spirit / Emerald Harmony) gives a Southeast Asia river alternative to the European rivers, embarking from Siem Reap, Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi, with a distinct "green season" (roughly July–October/December) that Scenic explicitly markets as a quieter, lower-crowd option.
- **New/incoming ships to track for future allotments**: Scenic river fleet expanding 13→16 ships by 2027–28 (names/rivers not yet detailed on official pages — **UNVERIFIED**); Scenic Eclipse III/"Ikon" ocean ship due 2028 (**UNVERIFIED** in detail); Emerald Astra (2026, Rhine/Main/Danube) and Emerald Lumi (2027, dedicated Seine ship) for river; Emerald Kaia (April 2026, Caribbean/Mediterranean/Adriatic), Emerald Raiya (2027, deployment **UNVERIFIED**) and Emerald Xara (2028, first Emerald yacht confirmed for Asia/Australia/South Pacific, with a possibly conflicting Caribbean 2027/28 season also cited — treat as **UNVERIFIED** pending clarification) for yachts.
- **No Egypt/Nile river product** was found for either brand on the official sites reviewed — if a client asks for a Nile cruise under these brands, this cannot be confirmed and should not be offered on the strength of this research.
- All season/duration information above is representative (drawn from named example itineraries on the official sites) — always reconfirm exact departure dates and current itinerary/port calls directly with the official site or the brand's trade desk before quoting, since river and yacht programmes are refreshed by season.
