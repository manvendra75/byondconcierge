# "Dream / Star Cruises" — Brand Research (Genting-legacy Asian cruise fleet)

Research date: 6 July 2026. Source policy: facts drawn **only** from the operating brands' official websites and their official press-release/media portals (`stardreamcruises.com` and its subdomain `media.stardreamcruises.com`). Search engines were used only to locate official URLs; no trade press, blogs or Wikipedia facts are used below. Anything not confirmable on an official site is explicitly marked **UNVERIFIED**. All dates/nights are itinerary patterns for orientation only — no prices or specific fixed departure dates are quoted beyond what is needed to identify a season.

## 1. Brand status & fleet — who operates what today

The client's shorthand "Dream / Star Cruises" corresponds, on official sources, to a single corporate umbrella: **StarDream Cruises**, officially launched 26 March 2025, operating website `stardreamcruises.com`. This is the **current legal successor** to the post-2022 "Resorts World Cruises" brand that briefly re-launched the Genting cruise fleet after the collapse of the original Genting Hong Kong / Star Cruises group. (Source: StarDream Cruises official press release, "A New Era Begins with the Grand Return of StarCruises and Dream Cruises" — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)

**Key official statement on lineage:** "Resorts World Cruises is proud and excited to announce the grand return of two of Asia's most iconic and legendary cruise brands – StarCruises (a refreshed take on the previous Star Cruises brand) and Dream Cruises. The Resorts World Cruises brand was used temporarily to restart cruising in Asia post Covid, and will be reverting to its original roots, which span over the last 30 years." (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)

StarDream Cruises today is a **parent umbrella for two distinct sub-brands**, each with its own ship class (Source: StarDream Cruises official — stardreamcruises.com, and press release boilerplate repeated across media.stardreamcruises.com releases):

| Sub-brand | Positioning (official) | Ship(s) | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dream Cruises** | "Redefines luxury cruising with expansive ships" | *Genting Dream* (flagship) | 3,352 lower-berth guests, 150,695 GT, 1,674 staterooms |
| **StarCruises** | "Affordable, lifestyle-driven cruising… intimate, mid-sized ships" | *Star Voyager*, *Star Navigator* | up to ~2,000 guests each |

**Fleet identity history (important — avoid confusing ship names across eras):**
- *Genting Dream* — debuted Singapore, 15 June 2022, under Resorts World Cruises; today sails as Dream Cruises' flagship, unchanged name throughout. (Source: StarDream Cruises official press release — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250121_RW1_Mumbai_Deployment_-_EN.pdf)
- *Resorts World One* — debuted Hong Kong/Taiwan, 10 March 2023 (75,338 GT, 1,856 lower-berth passengers), later homeported seasonally in Dubai (see §2) and briefly Singapore/Mumbai; **renamed Star Navigator** as part of the March 2025 rebrand and now sails under the StarCruises banner. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)
- *Star Scorpio* — a third ship due to join the fleet in Q1 2025; **renamed Star Voyager** before entering service (per official press-release title "Resorts World Cruises Renames Star Scorpio to Star Voyager", 7 February 2025). Also sails under StarCruises. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250207_PR_RWC_Star_Voyager_ENG.pdf; title referenced via official media portal)

**Current official fleet (as confirmed on stardreamcruises.com in 2026 materials) is exactly three ships: Genting Dream, Star Voyager, Star Navigator.** The "Resorts World Cruises" and "Resorts World One" names do not appear in current (2026) official deployment/press material — they are legacy/transition names only. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — stardreamcruises.com/en-us/destinations and stardreamcruises.com/en-us; and media.stardreamcruises.com/sdc-2026-highlights-bm.pdf)

**Crucial brand-status note for this brief:** the ship that previously ran the client's Dubai/Gulf season (*Resorts World One*) is the same hull now named **Star Navigator**, and is officially deployed to **Keelung, Taiwan** for a 10-month 2026 season (17 Feb–11 Dec 2026) sailing Japan and South Korea, with no Gulf itinerary listed. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20251003_SNA_Keelung_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)

## 2. 2026/27 deployment by region

### Singapore — Dream Cruises (*Genting Dream*)
*Genting Dream* is homeported in Singapore (Marina Bay Cruise Centre Singapore) **year-round**. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf; itinerary detail — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250930_GDR_2026.2027_SEA_PR_ENG.pdf)

Representative 2026/27 patterns (key ports · nights · season):
- Kuala Lumpur/Melaka or Port Klang · 2 nights · year-round (select Sunday departures)
- Penang & Kuala Lumpur, or Phuket · 3 nights · year-round (select Tuesday departures)
- Koh Samui & Pulau Redang · 4 nights · limited sailings, June and August 2026
- Bangkok (via Laem Chabang) & Koh Samui · 5 nights · June 2026 departure
- Bali (South & North) · 5 nights · December school-holiday departure (returns Dec 2026, following a first run in Nov 2025)

From 20 April 2026 to 29 March 2027, *Genting Dream* also adds select departures from **Port Klang** and year-round embarkation from **Melaka** (ICQS Terminal), sailing 3-night Singapore & Penang loops for Malaysian-market convenience. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250930_GDR_2026.2027_SEA_PR_ENG.pdf)

### Hong Kong — StarCruises (*Star Voyager*)
*Star Voyager* homeports in Hong Kong (Ocean Terminal, Tsim Sha Tsui) for a 9-month 2026 season, **13 February – 13 November 2026**. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20251027_SVO_HK_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)

Representative patterns:
- Xiamen · 2 nights · weekend departures throughout the season
- Xiamen, Ishigaki & Miyakojima (Okinawa) · 5 nights · several dates across the season
- Xiamen & Naha (Okinawa) · 5 nights · frequent sailings throughout the season

### Taiwan / Japan / South Korea — StarCruises (*Star Navigator*)
*Star Navigator* homeports in Keelung for a 10-month 2026 season, **17 February – 11 December 2026**, following a prior Kaohsiung deployment. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20251003_SNA_Keelung_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)

Representative patterns:
- Naha / Ishigaki (Okinawa) · 2–4 nights · frequent short sailings
- Kyushu & Okinawa (e.g. Kagoshima, Kumamoto or Fukuoka, Nagasaki + Naha) · 5 nights · select Sunday departures
- South Korea & Japan combined (Jeju or Busan, with Nagasaki/Kagoshima + Naha) · 5 nights · select Sunday departures
- Tokyo (via Yokohama), Kochi, Shimizu, Osaka (via Wakayama) · 7 nights · a single one-time sailing, 2 August 2026 (peak summer)

### Malaysia (Port Klang) — cross-brand
Port Klang serves as an additional 2026 homeport/embarkation point for both Dream Cruises (*Genting Dream*, first 2026 Port Klang departure 20 April 2026) and StarCruises (*Star Voyager* special sailings), positioned officially around "Visit Malaysia 2026." (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/sdc-2026-highlights-bm.pdf)

### Middle East / Gulf (Dubai and wider Arabian Gulf) — historical, status for 2026/27 UNVERIFIED
This is the region most relevant to a Dubai-based B2B site, so it is covered in the most depth. All facts below are from official Resorts World Cruises press releases (now archived on the current group's own media portal, media.stardreamcruises.com), which is the same corporate entity later rebranded.

- Maiden Dubai sailing: 1 November 2023 (referenced as the debut sailing in later official releases; the maiden-sailing release itself was not separately retrieved, so treat the exact date as **UNVERIFIED** pending direct confirmation, though the 6-month season announcement below confirms Dubai homeporting was underway from at least October 2024).
- Full announced season: **Resorts World One** homeported in **Dubai** (Hamdan bin Mohammed Cruise Terminal, Mina Rashid) for a 6-month season, **18 October 2024 – 13 April 2025**. (Source: Resorts World Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/1728037532133FIN20240513RW1ArabianGulfDeploymentENG&Arabic.pdf)
- Representative itinerary patterns during that season:
  - Sir Bani Yas (Abu Dhabi) · 2 nights · Friday departures, weekend-cruise format
  - Khasab & Muscat (Oman) · 3 nights (extendable to a 4-night round trip) · Sunday departures
  - Doha (Qatar) · 2 nights · Wednesday departures
  - Combined itineraries of 4, 5 or 7 nights were offered by joining two or three of the above legs.
  - During Qatar school holidays (24 October–2 November 2024), Doha additionally served as a secondary homeport, with 2-night Dubai and 5-night Dubai–Khasab–Muscat loops departing from Doha.
- The ship carried official **OIC/SMIIC Halal-Friendly Cruise Ship certification** for this Gulf deployment, with all meats UAE Halal-sourced, no pork served, gender-matched crew available on request for services such as spa, and bilingual (English/Arabic) onboard announcements. (Source: Resorts World Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/1728037532133FIN20240513RW1ArabianGulfDeploymentENG&Arabic.pdf)
- **This Gulf season did not run its full announced course.** The same ship (*Resorts World One*) was renamed *Star Navigator* in the March 2025 brand transition and, per the official transition release, was redeployed to an "exclusive 8-month operational period" in **Taiwan** starting 28 March 2025 — not back to Dubai. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)
- Searching the current official destinations pages, ship pages, the 2026/27 Genting Dream season release, the Hong Kong 2026 release, the Keelung 2026 release, and the StarDream Cruises "2026 Asia Experiences" highlights release (dated April 2026, the most recent comprehensive official deployment statement found) turned up **no mention of Dubai, the UAE, Qatar, Oman, Abu Dhabi, or any Middle East/Gulf itinerary for 2026 or 2027.** (Source: StarDream Cruises official — stardreamcruises.com/en-us/destinations; media.stardreamcruises.com/sdc-2026-highlights-bm.pdf)
- **Conclusion (UNVERIFIED as a positive claim, but well-supported as a negative one): no active or announced Gulf/Dubai homeport season for 2026/27 could be confirmed on the operator's official sources as of this research date (6 July 2026).** Agents should treat any claim of a current Dubai sailing under Dream Cruises / StarCruises / StarDream Cruises as unconfirmed and verify directly with the line before quoting it to clients.

### India (Mumbai) — UNVERIFIED for 2026

## 3. Positioning & clientele (as officially presented)

- Group tagline across all official releases: **"StarDream Cruises: The Cruise Line that Knows Asia Best."** (Source: StarDream Cruises official — repeated boilerplate across media.stardreamcruises.com releases, e.g. FIN_20251027_SVO_HK_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)
- **Dream Cruises** (Genting Dream) is positioned as the luxury tier — "a sophisticated and indulgent onboard experience," ships over 3,000 passengers, with **The Palace**, a ship-within-a-ship all-suite private enclave with 24-hour butler service. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)
- **StarCruises** (Star Voyager, Star Navigator) is positioned as the value/lifestyle tier — "affordable yet premium lifestyle cruising," mid-sized ships (~2,000 guests), "short port intensive itineraries for domestic and Fly-Cruise passengers." (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250226_PR_RWC_Transition_to_SC_&_DC_-_Eng.pdf)
- **Family positioning:** Genting Dream's official ship page describes a "Little Dreamers Club" for ages 2–12 alongside waterslides, a rope course/zipline, and family stateroom categories. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — stardreamcruises.com/en-us/our-ship/genting-dream)
- **Halal/Muslim-friendly positioning is officially documented and substantial, not incidental:**
  - Genting Dream is described in an official Dream Cruises release as "the world's first Muslim-friendly cruise," offering certified Halal cuisine and "a wide range of Muslim-friendly offerings." (Source: Dream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20251030_HTGA_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)
  - Dream Cruises has served for two consecutive years (2024, 2025) as official venue sponsor of the **HalalTrip Gastronomy Awards** Cook-Off Showdown, hosted onboard Genting Dream, in partnership with HalalTrip and the Singapore Halal Culinary Federation. (Source: Dream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20251030_HTGA_2026_PR_ENG.pdf)
  - Certified Halal and Jain vegetarian dining is called out as a standing feature "especially for Muslim travellers" in the April 2026 StarDream Cruises highlights release, across both StarCruises and Dream Cruises. (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/sdc-2026-highlights-bm.pdf)
  - During the historical Gulf deployment, the ship additionally held formal **OIC/SMIIC Halal-Friendly Cruise Ship certification** (see §2), a level of certification not mentioned in current Asia-deployment material — **UNVERIFIED whether this specific certification is maintained on the ship in its current (Star Navigator/Asia) deployment.**
- **Asian ethnic-market focus:** official material repeatedly emphasises "catering to diverse ethnic markets across Asia" and, historically for the Gulf season, "different ethnic and religious markets." (Source: Resorts World Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/FIN_20250121_RW1_Mumbai_Deployment_-_EN.pdf)
- Scale claim: StarDream Cruises states over 1.4 million inbound passenger arrivals into Malaysian ports and over 220,000 outbound passengers from Malaysia "since inception in 2022." (Source: StarDream Cruises official — media.stardreamcruises.com/sdc-2026-highlights-bm.pdf)

## 4. Notes for GCC agents

1. **Name the brand correctly and expect confusion.** The client's "Dream / Star Cruises" is the umbrella group **StarDream Cruises**, comprising two sold-separately sub-brands: **Dream Cruises** (Genting Dream, luxury tier) and **StarCruises** (Star Voyager and Star Navigator, value/lifestyle tier). These are not interchangeable in marketing copy — check which ship/brand a client itinerary actually uses before naming it.
2. **There is currently no confirmed Gulf/Dubai homeport product to sell for 2026/27.** The ship GCC clients may remember from Dubai sailings (marketed there as *Resorts World One*, under the *Resorts World Cruises* name) has since been renamed *Star Navigator* and is deployed to Taiwan/Japan/Korea for the 2026 season. No official source reviewed shows a return to Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha or Oman for 2026/27. Confirm directly with the operator before promising a local Gulf departure under any of these brand names.
3. **The realistic current GCC sell is fly-cruise into Asia**, not a Gulf homeport: Dream Cruises' Genting Dream sails Singapore year-round (with Malaysia/Thailand/Indonesia loops); StarCruises' two ships sail Hong Kong and Taiwan-based seasons into Japan/South Korea/Okinawa/China coast. Trade press (not cited here per source policy, but corroborating the official silence on Dubai) has reported the rebranded group actively courting the Gulf fly-cruise market for its Asia deployments — agents should independently verify any such sales-push claims with the line's own commercial team rather than relying on secondary coverage.
4. **Halal/Muslim-friendly credentials are a genuine, officially documented selling point**, especially for Dream Cruises' Genting Dream (certified Halal cuisine, Jain vegetarian options, HalalTrip Gastronomy Awards partnership, "world's first Muslim-friendly cruise" self-description). This travels well to GCC clientele even without a local homeport — it supports positioning an Asia fly-cruise package rather than a departure from home soil.
5. **Verify ship identity before quoting historic collateral.** Any older marketing material referencing "Resorts World Cruises," "Resorts World One," or booking domain `rwcruises.com` reflects the brand's 2022–2025 transitional identity; current official sales channels operate under `stardreamcruises.com`. Treat legacy collateral as superseded rather than current.
6. **India deployment status is unresolved.** A Mumbai homeport was announced for 2025, deferred officially to "2026," but no official 2026 confirmation was found — do not quote an India departure without direct operator confirmation.
