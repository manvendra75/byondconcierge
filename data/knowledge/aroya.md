# AROYA Cruises — Research Brief (for Dubai-based B2B / GCC trade audience)

**Research date:** 6 July 2026
**Source policy:** Official sources only — aroya.com (live booking engine and content pages, browsed directly) and cruisesaudi.com (Cruise Saudi corporate site, official parent/owner of AROYA Cruises). No trade press, no blogs, no Wikipedia. Anything not directly confirmable on these pages is marked **UNVERIFIED**. No prices and no specific departure dates are included per brief; itinerary timing is described only by month/season.

---

## 1. Fleet & ships

- AROYA Cruises currently operates a **single ship**, referred to on the official booking platform simply as **"Aroya"** (shown in the itinerary engine as *"AC01 by Aroya"*). No separate proper ship name (e.g. "MS/MV …") appears anywhere on the official pages browsed. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/aroya-ship; https://aroya.com/en)
- Current official specifications, as published on the ship page:
  - 18 decks
  - 1,678 cabins
  - Length: 335 metres
  - Tonnage: 160,000 tons
  - Maximum speed: 22 knots
  - Maximum passengers: 3,362 (lower berth)
  - Crew: 1,600+ members
  (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/aroya-ship)
- The homepage highlights repeat the same length/tonnage/speed/crew/passenger figures and add: "The first Arabian Cruise Line, Launched in 2024," plus cumulative brand stats of "+250,000 Guests Welcomed onboard Since Launch" and "25+ Ports of Call and Destinations." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en)
- **History, per Cruise Saudi's official media centre:**
  - 28 December 2023 — "AROYA Cruises Unveils its First Cruise Ship." This announcement describes the (then not-yet-refurbished) vessel as 335 metres long, with **19 decks and 1,682 cabins**, "currently undergoing an extensive refurbishment," with sailings from Jeddah confirmed to begin in 2024. (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/media-center/aroya-cruises-unveils-its-first-cruise-ship)
  - 28 June 2025 — "AROYA Cruises Embarks on its Mediterranean Season Maiden Voyage" (headline and date confirmed on the media-centre index; the article's own link was broken/404 on the official site at the time of research, so no further detail from this specific article could be verified). (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/media-center)
  - **UNVERIFIED / discrepancy note:** the pre-refurbishment figures in the Dec-2023 release (19 decks, 1,682 cabins) differ slightly from the current ship page (18 decks, 1,678 cabins) — consistent with changes made during the refurbishment, but the two official sources do not fully reconcile. Similarly, Cruise Saudi's corporate "Maritime" page states AROYA Cruises "was launched in 2023," while AROYA's own homepage states "Launched in 2024" — likely reflecting "ship unveiled" (2023) versus "first commercial sailings" (2024), but this is not explicitly reconciled on either official page.
- **Name meaning (official):** "AROYA" combines the words "Arabian" and "Roya" (Arabic for vision/dream). (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/maritime)
- **No second AROYA-branded ship is officially announced.** No fleet-expansion statement for AROYA itself was found on either official site.
- **Related but distinct Cruise Saudi vessel (not an AROYA ship):** Cruise Saudi's official "Cruise & Maritime" page describes **AMAN at Sea**, operated via "Neptune Co.," a joint venture between Cruise Saudi and Aman Group. This is an ultra-luxury 183-metre superyacht with 47 suites, dual-fuel powered (marine diesel oil and E-methanol), expected to sail from 2027, sailing the Mediterranean per the Cruise Saudi homepage. Cruise Saudi's site is explicit that AROYA Cruises and this superyacht venture "operate as entirely separate business units" — i.e., **AMAN at Sea is a sibling Cruise Saudi brand, not part of the AROYA fleet.** Worth flagging to GCC agents only as context on Cruise Saudi's wider portfolio, not as an AROYA product. (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/maritime; https://cruisesaudi.com)

## 2. 2026/27 deployment by region

Deployment below reflects what was live and bookable on AROYA's own search-and-book engine (aroya.com) at the time of research (6 July 2026), cross-checked against the official Red Sea and Mediterranean destination pages, which list the full official port rosters for each region.

### Red Sea programme from Jeddah (primary focus)

- **Home port:** Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.
- **Officially listed Red Sea ports of call** (per the Red Sea destination page): Jeddah, Ain Al Sokhna (Cairo), Sharm El Sheikh, Jabal Al-Sabaya Island (Saba Beach), Safaga (Hurghada), Marsa Alam (Port Ghalib), and Yanbu. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/destination-experience/red-sea)
- The Red Sea programme is officially branded **"Red Coral Secrets"** on this destination page, described as itineraries connecting "the cultures and ancient heritage of Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Jordan." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/destination-experience/red-sea)
- **Season observed via the live booking engine:** bookable Jeddah round-trip sailings ran from **September through May** (i.e., an autumn-to-spring season), with no Jeddah sailings shown for the summer months — consistent with a seasonal switch away from Gulf-region summer heat toward the Mediterranean (see below). (Source: AROYA official — live search results at https://aroya.com/en/search-listing, filtered to Port of Departure = Jeddah)
- **Representative itinerary patterns from Jeddah** (round trip unless noted; nights and routing only, no dates):
  - **2 nights** — a short "at sea" escape, round trip Jeddah with no port calls (a full day/night at sea).
  - **3 nights** — Jeddah – Jabal Al-Sabaya Island (Saba Beach); and separately, Jeddah – Yanbu.
  - **4 nights** — Jeddah – Sharm El Sheikh; and separately, Jeddah – Aqaba (marketed with the option to visit nearby Petra as a shore excursion).
  - **5 nights** — Jeddah – Sharm El Sheikh – Aqaba (round trip); one such sailing was explicitly branded as a "National Day Voyage" combining onboard National Day celebrations with the same routing.
  - **One-way 8 nights** — a repositioning itinerary: Istanbul – Marmaris – Bodrum – Port Said – Suez Canal transit – Sharm El Sheikh – Jeddah, explicitly bridging the Mediterranean and Red Sea seasons via the Suez Canal.
  (Source: AROYA official — live search results at https://aroya.com/en/search-listing, port = Jeddah and port = Istanbul)

### Mediterranean season from Istanbul

- **Home port:** Istanbul, Türkiye.
- **Officially listed Mediterranean ports of call** (per the Mediterranean destination page): Istanbul, Bodrum, Souda Bay, Mykonos, Piraeus (Athens), Marmaris, Kusadasi, Kas, Alexandria, and Rhodes. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/destination-experience/mediterranean)
- **Season observed via the live booking engine:** bookable Istanbul round-trip sailings were shown across the summer months (with instances found in July 2026 and June 2027), consistent with a June–September-type Mediterranean season, bookended by the Suez Canal repositioning voyages to/from Jeddah described above. (Source: AROYA official — live search results at https://aroya.com/en/search-listing, port = Istanbul)
- **Representative itinerary patterns from Istanbul** (round trip, 7 nights each):
  - Istanbul – Marmaris – Bodrum – Kas – Alexandria
  - Istanbul – Bodrum – Souda Bay – Mykonos – Piraeus (Athens)
  - Istanbul – Bodrum – Rhodes – Mykonos – Piraeus (Athens)
  (Source: AROYA official — live search results at https://aroya.com/en/search-listing, port = Istanbul)
- The Mediterranean season's official "maiden voyage" is dated 28 June 2025 per the Cruise Saudi media centre, though the linked article itself was not retrievable (see Section 1). (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/media-center)

### Arabian Gulf — officially supported, but no live sailings found at time of research

- AROYA's own booking engine explicitly lists **"Arabian Gulf"** as a selectable destination, and **Abu Dhabi, Dammam, Doha, and Dubai** as selectable Ports of Departure, alongside Jeddah, Istanbul, and Safaga. This confirms the Gulf is an official part of AROYA's route network structure. (Source: AROYA official — destination/port dropdowns on https://aroya.com/en and https://aroya.com/en/search-listing)
- However, when each of these Gulf ports (and Safaga) was searched individually on the live booking engine on 6 July 2026, **no on-sale itineraries were returned** — only Jeddah (Red Sea) and Istanbul (Mediterranean) had live, bookable sailings at the time of this research. **This is a point-in-time observation, not a confirmation that Gulf sailings do not exist or will not be offered** — it may reflect a seasonal gap, a booking window not yet opened, or a programme between announcements. Agents should check the live engine periodically for Gulf-departure sailings going on sale. **UNVERIFIED beyond this point:** any specific Gulf itinerary length, routing, or season, since none could be observed directly.
- Cruise Saudi's own destination-experience content separately promotes **Dammam** as "a dynamic coastal city on the Arabian Gulf" and gateway to the Al-Ahsa Oasis UNESCO World Heritage Site, as part of its wider destination portfolio — supporting that Gulf-side ports are part of the strategic plan, even though not currently reflected in AROYA's live sailings. (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com)

## 3. Positioning & clientele

- The tagline **"Remarkably Arabian"** is officially confirmed, appearing directly under the "AROYA Cruises" heading on Cruise Saudi's corporate "Cruise & Maritime" page. (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/maritime)
- The phrase recurs in the same spirit elsewhere: AROYA's own "Cruise 101" page states, "Cruise through remarkably Arabian waves…"; the current homepage hero headline reads, "A World of Experience Awaits you on Our Remarkable Arabian Waves"; and Cruise Saudi's Dec-2023 press release promises guests "a unique remarkably Arabian hospitality experience that reflects the warm and welcoming values of Saudi culture." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/aroya-cruise-101; https://aroya.com/en; Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/media-center/aroya-cruises-unveils-its-first-cruise-ship)
- **Ownership and mandate:** AROYA Cruises is owned by Cruise Saudi, which is itself Public Investment Fund-owned and was "launched in 2021" to build a full-suite cruise ecosystem in Saudi Arabia. Cruise Saudi's stated ambition is "to welcome 1.3 million cruise passengers to experience Saudi via sea by 2035." (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/about-us; https://cruisesaudi.com/maritime; https://cruisesaudi.com/media-center/aroya-cruises-unveils-its-first-cruise-ship)
- **Stated target guests:** Cruise Saudi's Maritime page describes AROYA as offering "Saudi nationals, expatriates, and regional guests a unique opportunity to explore the wonders of Saudi from the sea," built around brand values of "inspiration, enrichment, generosity and respect." (Source: Cruise Saudi official — https://cruisesaudi.com/maritime)
- **Family-friendly and faith-conscious features**, all confirmed directly on official pages:
  - A dedicated **nursery** for children aged 6 months to 24 months, and a minimum travel age of 6 months for all guests. (Source: AROYA official — FAQ, "Ship Facilities" and "Transportation, Boarding & Luggage" categories at https://aroya.com/en/faq)
  - A large **Kid Zone** (1,858 sqm, described as "one of the largest in the industry") plus a dedicated water park with five slides and a splash zone, and a wider children's/teens' programme spanning a Nursery, Discoverers' Zone, Captains' Zone, Teens Club, Sailors' Bridge, Climbing Wall, Chefs Academy, Sailors' Workshop, and Sailors' Stage. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/aroya-ship; https://aroya.com/en/things-to-do)
  - **"Exclusive Places for Women"**: the ship page states there are "exclusive venues and dedicated hours for female guests including adults pool, gym, thermal spa, and separate prayer room." The FAQ specifies that women-only pool/spa/gym hours run from **9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. in the Sky Pool while sailing from Jeddah**, with hours varying by itinerary (guests advised to check the onboard Daily Programme). (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/aroya-ship; FAQ "Ship Facilities" category at https://aroya.com/en/faq)
  - **Prayer facilities**: separate male and female prayer rooms are located on Deck 5; prayer mats are provided in all cabins; guests are advised to use a Qibla app given the ship's changing orientation depending on the itinerary. A "Prayer Room" is also listed as a standalone onboard experience on the Things To Do page. (Source: AROYA official — FAQ "Ship Facilities" category at https://aroya.com/en/faq; https://aroya.com/en/things-to-do)
  - **Family cabin option**: a "Premier Family Suite" is offered within the Khuzama Suites collection, described as "a retreat where family moments are elevated… This suite offers you and your family a home at sea." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/khuzama-experience)
  - Shisha is officially confirmed as available at four named venues: Legends Lounge (Deck 8), Beats (Deck 17), Sky Pool & Lounge (Deck 18), and Al Matal Terrace (Deck 19). (Source: AROYA official — FAQ "Ship Facilities" category at https://aroya.com/en/faq)
  - ADA-accessible Balcony Cabins are offered for guests with mobility needs. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/cabins)
  - A dedicated **MICE & Charters** offering targets business/incentive groups, positioned as blending "business and leisure" with "thoughtfully designed meeting spaces and world-class venues." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en; https://aroya.com/en/mice)

## 4. Notes for GCC agents

- **Embarkation documents**, per AROYA's official FAQ ("Policies & Documents" category):
  - For **round-trip Red Sea sailings that start and end in Jeddah with no international port calls**, guests need only a **KSA National ID or KSA Iqama** — no passport is required.
  - More generally, the FAQ states: "The required documents for boarding the ship include a passport for international trips and a national ID/Iqama for local trips within Saudi Arabia. Residents must also ensure they have valid exit/re-entry visas." (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/faq)
  - **UNVERIFIED:** the official FAQ frames this specifically around Saudi (KSA) IDs/Iqamas; it does not explicitly confirm whether non-Saudi GCC-national identity cards (e.g., UAE Emirates ID) are accepted in place of a passport for the same domestic Jeddah round trips. Agents should confirm this directly with AROYA/Cruise Saudi for non-Saudi GCC nationals.
- **Visas generally:** the FAQ states simply that "guests must ensure that all visas are valid before departure." For itineraries calling at Türkiye (i.e., Mediterranean sailings ex-Istanbul), the FAQ notes that visa requirements "depend on nationality" and points guests to Turkey's official e-Visa portal (www.evisa.gov.tr). (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/faq)
- AROYA's site maintains standalone policy pages for **"Visa Service Terms and Conditions"** and **"Flights Transport Terms and Conditions,"** indicating the line (or its booking partners) offers visa- and flight-related booking services alongside the cruise itself. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/visa-booking-request; https://aroya.com/en/flight-transport-terms)
- **Boarding:** the FAQ states only that "passengers must arrive at the port at the specified time for boarding as mentioned in their transportation contract or itinerary" — no fixed universal check-in window is published beyond this. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/faq)
- **Minimum age for travel:** 6 months. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/faq)
- **Official trade-partner network:** AROYA's site includes a dedicated "Our Travel Trade Partners" directory with a country selector explicitly covering all **six GCC states** (Bahrain, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, and UAE), alongside several other markets (Germany, Malta, Tunisia, Türkiye, Algeria, Brunei, Egypt, India, Italy, Malaysia, Russia, Morocco, Singapore, South Africa). This confirms an active, officially listed trade network spanning the GCC, plus a separate trade **Partners Portal** login (portal.aroya.com) for agent bookings. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en/travel-partners)
- **Currency support:** the booking engine supports SAR, USD, AED, EUR, and GBP at checkout — AED support is directly relevant for UAE-based agents and clients. (Source: AROYA official — currency selector on https://aroya.com/en)
- **Contact channels:** the official contact number is +966 920022277, with a WhatsApp channel (wa.me/966920022277) also listed site-wide as an official contact/support route. (Source: AROYA official — https://aroya.com/en, footer/header)
