# MSC Cruises — Research Brief for GCC Travel Agents

*Compiled for a Dubai-based B2B travel platform serving GCC agents. All facts below are drawn exclusively from MSC Cruises' official websites (msccruises.com international/English pages, browsed directly — fleet, ship and destination pages). Where MSC's own site did not state a fact, it is marked **UNVERIFIED**. No prices or specific departure dates are included; only seasons/months, as requested.*

**A note on source access:** MSC's live site blocks automated fetching from this research environment (network-level 403), so pages were retrieved via the Internet Archive Wayback Machine's cached copies of the same official msccruises.com/int URLs (snapshots dated February–April 2026, i.e. current at time of writing). URLs cited below are the original MSC URLs; content reflects what MSC itself published at those addresses.

**Important caveat for GCC agents (flagged, not sourced to MSC):** the official Dubai/Abu Dhabi/Doha destination page (last verified snapshot, March 2026) still actively markets the "Emirates Cruises 2026–2027" Arabian Gulf season with MSC Euribia. Separately, the main destinations-overview navigation page (also March 2026) lists Mediterranean, Northern Europe, Canary Islands & Madeira, Caribbean & Antilles, Ocean Cay, Bermuda, Alaska, South Africa, South America, Asia, Grand Voyages, Panama Canal and World Cruises — but does **not** include Dubai/Arabian Gulf as a menu entry in that snapshot, even though the dedicated Dubai page still exists at its own URL. This research could not access MSC's live site to check current 2026/27 Gulf booking status directly (a strict requirement of this brief was to use only MSC's own site, and no more recent official snapshot was retrievable). **UNVERIFIED**: whether the Arabian Gulf winter 2026/27 season is still operating as marketed — agents should verify current Gulf sailing status directly with MSC or a GDS/CRS before quoting clients, independent of what is written below.

## 1. Fleet & ships

MSC Cruises describes itself as the world's third-largest cruise brand and the market leader (by share and deployed capacity) in Europe, South America and Southern Africa, with a strong and fast-growing presence in the Caribbean, North America and the Far East. The company is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, part of the privately held MSC Group, and employs over 30,000 staff globally (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).

The official "About MSC" page states the current fleet is **22 modern ships**, "projected to grow to 24 cruise ships with two new ships to come into service between 2025 and 2026" (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc). The official ships browse page separately lists **25 named vessels** (current ships plus confirmed newbuilds already marketed): MSC Armonia, Bellissima, Divina, Euribia, Fantasia, Grandiosa, Lirica, Magnifica, Meraviglia, Musica, Opera, Orchestra, Poesia, Preziosa, Seascape, Seashore, Seaside, Seaview, Sinfonia, Splendida, Virtuosa, World America, World Asia, World Atlantic and World Europa (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/ships).

### MSC Yacht Club

The MSC Yacht Club is marketed as an exclusive "ship within a ship" — private key-card areas with a dedicated restaurant, lounge (Top Sail Lounge), sundeck and pool, 24/7 butler and concierge service, premium drinks package, unlimited Wi-Fi and complimentary Thermal Suite access in the MSC Aurea Spa (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/msc-yacht-club). It spans 11 suite types fleet-wide, though not every type is on every ship:
- All ships: Deluxe Suites with balcony.
- Newest vessels: Royal Suites with separate bedroom and private whirlpool.
- Meraviglia-class ships: Duplex Suites with whirlpool and front-facing views.
- Seaside-class ships (MSC Seashore, MSC Seascape): Owner's Suites and Grand Suites.
- World Class ships: Owner's and Duplex Suites (with/without whirlpool) plus Interior Suites.
- MSC World Asia and MSC World Atlantic exclusively: a Royal Duplex Suite sleeping up to 6 guests across two decks.
- The Yacht Club Grill & Bar is available specifically on Meraviglia, Seaside and World Class ships.
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/msc-yacht-club)

MSC's own homepage (as captured) also flags a newly unveiled MSC Yacht Club aboard MSC Orchestra (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int).

### The Gulf-deployed ship

MSC's official Dubai/Emirates destination FAQ page references guest arrangements around "MSC Euribia's departure" from the Doha cruise terminal, confirming **MSC Euribia** (Meraviglia Plus Class, LNG-powered) as the ship operating MSC's Dubai–Abu Dhabi–Doha winter season referenced on that page (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).

Historically, MSC's own corporate history page records that **MSC Bellissima** was the first MSC ship deployed to the region: it entered service in March 2019 on Western Mediterranean itineraries "before being deployed to Dubai, United Arab Emirates in the autumn" of that year (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).

## 2. Worldwide 2026/27 deployment by region

### Arabian Gulf — Dubai, Abu Dhabi & Doha (home market — extra depth)

MSC markets this as "Emirates Cruises 2026–2027," operating from **three home ports**: Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Doha (Qatar) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).

**Season:** MSC states its Emirates cruises "sail from November through March," calling this the region's best-weather window (daytime 24–31°C, evenings 14–20°C) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).

**Dubai embarkation point:** MSC ships dock at **Port Rashid (Mina Rashid)** — specifically the **Hamdan Bin Mohammed Cruise Terminal 3**, address: "Hamdan Bin Mohammed Cruise Terminal 3, P.O. Box 6769, Port Rashid, Dubai, UAE" (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai/united-arab-emirates/dubai).

**Ports of call across the season:** Sir Bani Yas Island (UAE, offshore nature reserve roughly midway between Abu Dhabi and Qatar), Manama (Bahrain), and two Omani calls — Muscat and Khasab (Musandam exclave, ~200 km north of Dubai) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).

**Representative itinerary patterns (official FAQ):**
1. **7-night round trip**, most common pattern, typically ex-Dubai, taking in Abu Dhabi, Doha, Bahrain and Sir Bani Yas Island.
2. **6-night round trip** departing Abu Dhabi, calling at Dubai, Bahrain and Doha.
3. **3-night short break** from Abu Dhabi to Dubai, including a day at Sir Bani Yas Island.
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai)

**Fly-cruise access:** MSC's official Fly-Cruise packages for this season are sold from UK/Ireland airports — London Heathrow, Birmingham, Manchester and Dublin — with all flights landing in Abu Dhabi (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai). No GCC-origin fly-cruise routing is stated on the page reviewed.

**MSC World Cruise 2028 also calls at Dubai**, with a confirmed **overnight stay in Dubai** as one of only seven overnight calls on the entire 115-night voyage (see Grand Voyages/World Cruise section below) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-world-cruise-2028).

### Mediterranean — year-round core market

MSC's "About MSC" page confirms its ships "sail year-round in the Mediterranean and the Caribbean" (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc). MSC is positioned as market leader in Europe overall.

**Home ports (official Mediterranean page):** Barcelona and Valencia (Spain); Marseille and Cannes (France); Genoa, Civitavecchia (Rome), Naples, Venice, Messina, and others across the Italian coast; Piraeus (Athens, Greece) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/mediterranean).

**Season/best-time framing:** MSC states Mediterranean cruises run year-round; it recommends spring (March–June) or autumn (September–November) for milder weather, and summer for the fullest cultural/beach combination, plus Christmas/New Year sailings (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/mediterranean).

**Representative itinerary patterns:**
1. **Western Mediterranean** — Barcelona, Valencia (Spain), Civitavecchia/Rome, Genoa/Portofino, Naples/Pompeii/Capri/Sorrento (Italy), Marseille/Provence and Cannes/Monte Carlo (France).
2. **Eastern Mediterranean** — Venice, Split and Dubrovnik (Croatia), Istanbul (Turkey), Piraeus/Athens and the Greek islands (Corfu, Santorini, Mykonos, Rhodes, Heraklion/Crete).
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/mediterranean)

MSC's newest World Class ship, **MSC World Asia**, is confirmed sailing the Mediterranean from Winter 2026 (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/mediterranean; https://www.msccruises.com/int).

### Northern Europe — summer season

**Home ports:** Southampton (UK, for Baltic, Fjords and North Atlantic itineraries); also Le Havre and Cherbourg (France); Ijmuiden near Amsterdam (Netherlands); Hamburg and Kiel (Germany); Copenhagen (Denmark); Rotterdam and Zeebrugge (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/northern-europe).

**Season:** MSC's own FAQ states "the best time of the year is in late spring and summer," when temperatures range roughly 12–22°C (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/northern-europe).

**Representative itinerary patterns:**
1. **Norwegian Fjords** — Geirangerfjord (Hellesylt) and Nærøyfjord (Flåm, both UNESCO sites), plus Ålesund/Olden, Måløy, Stavanger, Ålandsnes in the south and Tromsø, Alta, Hammerfest in the north.
2. **Baltic capitals** — MSC states MSC Poesia specifically offers **7- or 11-night** Baltic cruises from **May to September**, calling at Germany, Denmark, Finland, Sweden, Poland, Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia (roughly 10 hours in each port) — Tallinn, Riga, Stockholm, Helsinki, Copenhagen and Visby (Gotland) are named calls.
3. **British Isles / North Atlantic** — ex-Southampton, calling Cork/Ringaskiddy (Ireland) and Belfast (Northern Ireland), extending on further itineraries to Greenland (Qaqortoq, Nuuk, Ilulissat) and Iceland (Akureyri, Ísafjörður).
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/northern-europe)

### Caribbean (including Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve)

**Home ports:** New York, Miami and Port Canaveral (Orlando), Florida; Bridgetown, Barbados, for Southern Caribbean sailings; from November 2026, new itineraries from **La Romana**, Dominican Republic (featuring Catalina Island) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/caribbean-and-antilles).

**Season/length:** MSC's FAQ states cruises run **3 to 21 nights**, with the region's dry season (November–March) most popular, though sailings run year-round (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/caribbean-and-antilles).

**Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve:** MSC's exclusive private island in the Bahamas — described as "the biggest island development by any cruise company in the Caribbean" with "eight pristine beaches, spread over two sandy miles" (named beaches include Sunset Beach, North Beach, South Beach, Paradise Sands Beach, Lighthouse Bay Beach, Bimini Beach, Seakers Cove and the Yacht-Club-exclusive Ocean House Beach). It is reachable from Miami, Port Canaveral and New York, and MSC states "all ships sailing a Bahamas or Western Caribbean itinerary will visit Ocean Cay" (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/caribbean-and-antilles/bahamas/ocean-cay-msc-marine-reserve).

**Representative itinerary patterns:**
1. **Bahamas/Ocean Cay** short cruises ex-Miami or Port Canaveral, calling Nassau and Ocean Cay.
2. **Western Caribbean** ex-Miami, calling Cozumel and Costa Maya (Mexico), Georgetown (Cayman Islands), Ocho Rios (Jamaica).
3. **Southern Caribbean** ex-Bridgetown (Barbados).
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/caribbean-and-antilles)

### South America

**Home ports/major calls:** Rio de Janeiro, Santos (São Paulo), Salvador, Búzios and Camboriú (Brazil); Montevideo and Punta del Este (Uruguay); Buenos Aires (Argentina) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/south-america).

### South Africa

**Home ports:** Cape Town and Durban (MSC opened a dedicated Nelson Mandela Cruise Terminal in Durban in November 2021, per MSC's own history page), plus Port Elizabeth (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/south-africa; https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).

**Ports of call:** Walvis Bay and Lüderitz (Namibia); Port Louis (Mauritius); Nosy Be and Antsiranana (Madagascar); La Possession (Réunion); Pomene and Portuguese Island/Inhaca (Mozambique) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/south-africa).

MSC is positioned as market leader in Southern Africa (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc). The official South Africa page did not state explicit season months or standard night-lengths on the copy reviewed. **UNVERIFIED**: precise season window for the standalone South Africa domestic programme (the region connects into the Grand Voyages repositioning route toward the Mediterranean via Réunion and Mauritius — see below).

### Asia

**Home ports:** Tokyo/Yokohama and Keelung (Taiwan) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/asia).

**Countries covered:** Japan, Taiwan (China) and the Republic of Korea, with Singapore named as a future call point via the MSC World Cruise 2028 (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/asia; https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-world-cruise-2028).

**Representative itinerary patterns:**
1. **Japan circumnavigation** — Kagoshima to Hokkaido, calling Beppu, Naha/Okinawa.
2. **Japan–Taiwan** — via the Okinawa islands (Naha, Ishigaki, Miyako) to Keelung, near Taipei.
3. **Republic of Korea extension** — from Japan sailing to Busan and Jeju Island.
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/asia)

The official Asia destination page did not state explicit season months, standard night-lengths, or which specific ship is deployed there. **UNVERIFIED**: exact Asia season window and deployed ship name (not confirmed on the pages reviewed).

### MSC Grand Voyages (transatlantic/repositioning)

MSC's official Grand Voyages page states that for 2026 there are **37 transatlantic and repositioning cruises across 17 different ships**, visiting over **35 countries and 100 ports**, with each individual Grand Voyage lasting **10 to 30 days** (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-grand-voyages).

**Representative itinerary patterns:**
1. **Transatlantic** — sailing historic European ports (Venice, Genoa, Barcelona, Copenhagen, Hamburg) via Mallorca, Casablanca and the Canary Islands to the Caribbean, or on to Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Buenos Aires in South America.
2. **Repositioning (South Africa ↔ Mediterranean)** — routes between Durban/Cape Town and the Mediterranean via Réunion and Mauritius.
(Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-grand-voyages)

MSC Virtuosa is specifically promoted for a Grand Voyage crossing (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-grand-voyages).

### MSC World Cruises 2027 & 2028 (both aboard MSC Magnifica)

**MSC World Cruise 2027:** 121 nights/122 days, departing **Genoa each January**, covering **25 countries** with **seven overnight calls**. Route: Genoa → Valparaiso (36 nights/37 days) → Sydney (34 nights/35 days) → back to Genoa (51 nights/52 days). MSC offers 6 embarkation ports so guests can book individual segments rather than the full voyage (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-world-cruise-2027).

**MSC World Cruise 2028:** 115 nights, **29 countries**, described as spanning "Mediterranean charm to Pacific horizons," crossing the Panama Canal, and covering the Caribbean and Central America, South America and the Pacific Ocean, Australia (Sydney, Cairns), Southeast Asia and the Indian Ocean, and the **Arabian Peninsula and Red Sea** — departing and finishing in **Genoa**, and including a passage through the Suez Canal via Alexandria back to the Mediterranean. It includes 15 free excursions and **seven overnight calls**: Callao/Lima (Peru), Papeete (Tahiti), Sydney and Cairns (Australia), Ha Long Bay/Hanoi (Vietnam), Singapore, Mumbai (India) and — notably for this market — **Dubai (United Arab Emirates)** (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-world-cruise-2028).

## 3. Positioning & clientele

- **Scale and market leadership:** "World's third-largest cruise brand," and the stated leader (by market share and deployed capacity) in Europe, South America and Southern Africa, with fast growth in the Caribbean, North America and Far East (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).
- **European heritage narrative:** MSC frames itself around "a centuries-old relationship with the sea," tracing its Aponte family origins back over 300 years in Sorrento, Italy, positioning MSC Cruises as "now the largest privately owned cruise company in the world," part of the Geneva-headquartered MSC Group (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).
- **"European style" product cues:** MSC World Atlantic is explicitly marketed as blending "European design with American comfort," and the site repeatedly frames the fleet's design language, dining and service culture as continental-European in inspiration (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int; https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).
- **Brand tagline / "Art of Cruising":** current homepage messaging centres on "Discover the best holiday ever" ("When the perfect holiday is planned, the magic lives in the unplanned"), and MSC World Asia is promoted under "The Art of Cruising" — "blending refined craftsmanship with meticulous attention to detail" (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int).
- **Always-included basics (as marketed):** full-board dining (breakfast/lunch/dinner in main restaurants and buffet), "world-class entertainment" (Broadway-style theatres, live international acts), a Kids Club with daily family activities, and gym/pool/sport facilities with free access (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int).
- **Families:** MSC frames itself as family-friendly across the fleet, with Kids' Clubs and aquapark/entertainment for all ages (SportPlex, bumper cars) called out on multiple ship and destination pages (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/msc-yacht-club).
- **MSC Yacht Club / premium tier:** positioned as "the luxury cruise experience — exclusivity and privacy in a world of choice," an "authentic ship within a ship" with 24/7 butler and concierge, private restaurant/lounge/pool/sundeck, all-inclusive premium drinks and unlimited Wi-Fi, and complimentary access to the Thermal Suite in the MSC Aurea Spa (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/msc-yacht-club).
- **Sustainability:** MSC states a long-term goal of net-zero greenhouse gas emissions for marine operations by 2050, cites ISO certifications (50001 energy, 14001 environmental, 22000 food safety, 45001 safety, 21070 garbage management, 9001 management systems), and highlights its transformation of Ocean Cay from a former industrial sand-excavation site into a marine reserve, including removal of 7,500 tons of scrap metal and planting of over 75,000 plants/shrubs (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/ships; https://www.msccruises.com/int/about-msc).
- **Explora Journeys:** MSC's official navigation consistently cross-links to "Explora Journeys" as a separate, adjacent luxury brand within the same group — relevant to note when positioning MSC Cruises itself as the mainstream/premium-mass segment rather than the ultra-luxury segment (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int).

## 4. Notes for GCC agents

- **Embarkation port:** MSC's Dubai calls dock at **Port Rashid (Mina Rashid)**, specifically the **Hamdan Bin Mohammed Cruise Terminal 3** (address: P.O. Box 6769, Port Rashid, Dubai, UAE) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai/united-arab-emirates/dubai).
- **Distances/transfers (as stated by MSC):** the terminal is around 9 km from Old Dubai (souks, Dubai Museum, Dubai Creek) and within 20 km of Jumeirah Beach/downtown Dubai (Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall). Dubai International Airport is roughly 16 km away; MSC recommends its own MSC transfer service (booked via the MSC call centre) or taxi (MSC estimates around half an hour, "around £30"). The nearest metro station is Al Ghubaiba on the Green Line, a roughly 30-minute walk or short taxi ride from the terminal (change at Union coming from the airport, or at Burjuman coming from Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall/Dubai Marina) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai/united-arab-emirates/dubai).
- **Doha terminal practicalities:** MSC's own Dubai/Emirates FAQ states the **Doha Cruise Terminal opens at 8:30am**, with no port-area access before that for security reasons; Fly & Cruise guests are met by MSC/Discover Qatar representatives after baggage claim and taken to the B12 Beach Club to wait. It also notes that under local regulations, once inside the Doha cruise terminal a guest is considered to have left the country and cannot re-enter to visit the city (with a stated exception around one specific overnight-in-Doha departure referencing MSC Euribia) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).
- **Currencies and time zones (as stated by MSC):** UAE dirham (AED), Qatari riyal (QAR), Bahraini dinar (BHD), Omani rial (OMR). UAE and Oman run on Gulf Standard Time (GST, GMT+4); Qatar and Bahrain run on Arabian Standard Time (AST, GMT+3) (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).
- **UK/Ireland fly-cruise routing only, as stated:** the official Fly & Cruise packages named on MSC's Dubai page depart from London Heathrow, Birmingham, Manchester and Dublin, landing in Abu Dhabi — no GCC-origin fly-cruise packaging is mentioned on the page reviewed, which agents should note when building outbound-from-GCC propositions (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/dubai).
- **Regional locale:** MSC's own country/locale selector includes "United Arab Emirates | English" and "Qatar | English" and "Saudi Arabia | English" as selectable regional storefronts on msccruises.com — but there is **no separate msccruises.ae domain** (DNS lookup returns non-existent domain); the UAE-facing content runs on msccruises.com's own locale system rather than a dedicated country domain (verified directly via DNS lookup during this research, not an MSC-published claim).
- **Booking-status caveat:** as flagged above, this brief could not confirm current live 2026/27 Gulf-season booking availability directly on MSC's site (source-access limits in this research environment); agents should verify directly with MSC/GDS before advising clients, since the Gulf season is a moving target year to year (MSC Bellissima in 2019, MSC Euribia referenced for the season captured here).
- **Dubai as a headline call, not just a home port:** beyond the seasonal Gulf programme, Dubai also appears as a marquee **overnight port of call** on the long-haul MSC World Cruise 2028 — useful as an ancillary hook (attracting global repositioning-cruise guests through Dubai) distinct from the core winter Gulf season (Source: MSC official — https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/msc-world-cruise-2028).
