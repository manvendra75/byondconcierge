# Celebrity Cruises

_Official source: https://www.celebritycruises.com/_
_Captured: 2026-07-15_

All claims below are drawn from Celebrity's own official website (celebritycruises.com) only. No third-party sources used. Prices and exact departure dates deliberately omitted per brief.

## Positioning

Celebrity positions itself as an **award-winning premium cruise line** — an "elevated premium vacation experience", explicitly a step above mass-market cruising while remaining approachable. It is not marketed as "ultra-luxury" but as premium/elevated.

- Homepage hero headline: **"You'll Never Want to Vacation Any Other Way"**
- Brand essence phrases used on site: "elevated premium vacation experience", "Nautical Miles Ahead of Any Other Vacation", "every detail elevated beyond expectations".
- Core value proposition: the "intimate feel and thoughtful service of small ships, with the variety and excitement of bigger ones."
- Describes itself as an "industry pioneer" (35 years) offering "experiences you won't find anywhere else."
- Ships cited as **Forbes Travel Guide Four-Star Rated**; guest-to-staff ratio of nearly 2:1.
- Source: https://www.celebritycruises.com/ and https://www.celebritycruises.com/whats-included

## Signature features

Per the official onboard pages, Celebrity markets:

- **The Retreat** — its top suite class (all-inclusive luxury within the ship). Includes a dedicated butler, the exclusive **Retreat Lounge** (complimentary beverages, gourmet bites, dedicated concierge, available 24/7), a private **Retreat Sundeck**, and **Luminae** — a private restaurant for suite guests with signature dishes from chef **Daniel Boulud**. (Retreat Sundeck available on Edge Series and "Revolutionized" ships; noted as not available on Solstice, Eclipse, Reflection, Constellation, Infinity.)
- **The Magic Carpet** — a cantilevered, floating platform reaching 13 storeys above sea level, with seating, a full bar and live music; unique to the Edge Series.
- **Eden** — an immersive multi-storey venue combining the Eden Restaurant with entertainment.
- **Dining programme** — chef partnerships (Daniel Boulud) plus signature venues: Le Voyage, Eden Restaurant, Fine Cut Steakhouse, Le Petit Chef at Qsine, Rooftop Garden Grill, Lawn Club Grill.
- **The Lawn Club / Rooftop Garden / Resort Deck** — real-grass open-air lawn and rooftop leisure/dining spaces.
- **Spa & wellness** — the **SEA Thermal Suite** (also referenced as the Persian Garden), Solarium, Fitness Center, salon and barber.
- **Grand Plaza** — central three-deck social hub; plus The Club nightlife venue.
- Luxury retail (Bvlgari, Cartier, Tiffany & Co., Montblanc) and art galleries.
- Source: https://www.celebritycruises.com/things-to-do-onboard and https://www.celebritycruises.com/things-to-do-onboard/staterooms/suite-class

## Arabian Gulf / Dubai

**NO — Celebrity does not currently sail the Arabian Gulf, Dubai or Abu Dhabi.** This is stated explicitly and consistently across the official port/destination pages.

- Dubai port page: "Please Note: While we don't currently sail to Dubai, you can still discover the beauty of the region on one of our Asia cruises." (https://www.celebritycruises.com/ports/dubai)
- Abu Dhabi port page: "Please Note: While we don't currently sail to Abu Dhabi, you can still discover the beauty of the region on one of our Asia cruises." (https://www.celebritycruises.com/ports/abu-dhabi)
- Arabian Sea / Dubai & India page: "While we don't currently sail to the Arabian Sea, you can discover the beauty of Asia on one of our Asia cruises." (https://www.celebritycruises.com/destinations/dubai-india-cruises/arabian-sea-cruises)

Important nuance for GCC agents: Celebrity **maintains destination/port guide pages** for Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Muscat and the "Dubai & India / Arabian Sea" concept (describing Port Rashid facilities, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, desert safaris, ports such as Muscat, Mumbai, Goa, Cochin and potential calls in Sri Lanka/Thailand, with Celebrity Constellation cited as an Asia ship). These read as legacy/aspirational guides. However, every one of these pages carries the explicit disclaimer that Celebrity does **not currently sail** there. No live Gulf embarkation, turnaround or port-of-call itinerary is offered on the site as captured. Do not represent Celebrity as currently sailing from/to Dubai or Abu Dhabi.

## What's included

Celebrity's **"All Included"** fare model bundles three core elements (prices stripped per brief):

1. **Classic Drinks Package** — beers, spirits, cocktails, liqueurs, frozen drinks, speciality coffees and organic teas, bottled water, Coca-Cola selections and wines by the glass (up to the classic tier serving value). Upgradeable to the Premium Beverage Package.
2. **Basic Wi-Fi** — unlimited basic internet for web browsing, email and text messaging. Upgradeable to Premium Wi-Fi.
3. **Tips Included** — stateroom, waiter, assistant waiter and headwaiter gratuities, per Celebrity's suggested guidelines.

Also standard in every fare (not part of the "All Included" three but included on all cruises): accommodation with twice-daily housekeeping (makeup and turndown), complimentary main dining (breakfast/lunch/dinner across multiple venues incl. Oceanview Cafe and Mast Grill), theatre productions and live entertainment, fitness centre and select fitness classes, pools and outdoor spaces, and family programmes (Camp at Sea, Teen Club).

Suite (The Retreat) and Galápagos (Flora) fares are more fully all-inclusive (e.g. Galápagos includes all onboard drinks, gratuities, park fees and guided excursions).
- Source: https://www.celebritycruises.com/things-to-do-onboard/onboard-packages/all-included, https://www.celebritycruises.com/whats-included, https://www.celebritycruises.com/faqs/all-included

## Loyalty

**Captain's Club** — Celebrity's rewards programme. Six tiers by accumulated Club Points:

- **Preview** (pre-sailing) — Loyalty Desk access, newsletter
- **Classic** (2+ points)
- **Select** (150+ points)
- **Elite** (300+ points) — complimentary laundry, spa benefits, Captain's Club cocktail hour
- **Elite Plus** (750+ points) — speciality dining discounts, additional complimentary laundry, more
- **Zenith** (3,000+ points) — top tier: access to the Retreat Lounge / Michael's Club, complimentary premium beverage package, complimentary premium Wi-Fi, complimentary laundry

Points are earned per night based on stateroom category. A Royal Caribbean loyalty status match is offered. General benefits scale by tier: onboard events, food & beverage perks, internet and photo savings, stateroom upgrades and access to exclusive voyages.
- Source: https://www.celebritycruises.com/captains-club and https://www.celebritycruises.com/captains-club/tiers-and-benefits
