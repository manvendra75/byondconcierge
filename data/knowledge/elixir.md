# Elixir Cruises — Research Brief (Official Site Only)

Source policy: all claims below are drawn exclusively from the official website **elixir-cruises.com** (browsed directly — homepage, about-us, ship pages, itinerary pages, Red Sea page, contact/rates pages). No trade press, blogs, or Wikipedia were used for facts. Anything not confirmable on the official site is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

**Important disclosure on brand structure:** "Elixir Cruises" markets itself as a distinct boutique brand, but its own site states in its footer/contact text that it is the "General Sales Agent [for] Yacht Cruise Company®" (Rotterdam, Netherlands), which in turn works with "more than 30 partners — cruise companies operating with customised vessels" worldwide (all under 300 passengers). In practice this means Elixir sells both its own two named yachts (Elysium, Gemaya) and third-party partner-operator sailings (e.g. its Red Sea product) through one portal. This should be borne out clearly in any content page — it is not a single-fleet operator in the conventional sense.
(Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/about-us; https://www.elixir-cruises.com/contact)

**Two-domain note:** a second, apparently related domain, **elixir.cruises**, also presents itself as "Elixir Boutique Cruises" but could not be verified for this brief (the domain blocked automated access). Only elixir-cruises.com content is used below. Anyone publishing on this brand should manually check elixir.cruises in a real browser before finalising copy, in case it holds materially different or newer information.

## 1. Fleet & vessels

Elixir's own named fleet comprises two small yachts, both marketed as "boutique cruise ships":

- **Elysium** — length overall 63.8m, breadth 12.5m, draught 3.42m, gross tonnage 1,610T, cruising speed 11 knots, 2 lifeboats. 25 cabins across 5 categories (10–20 sqm). Guests: 50. Crew: 24–28.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/the-yacht-elysium)
- **Gemaya** — 33 metres long, 5 cabins/suites only (named suites, 14–27 sqm, accommodating 1–3 guests each), crew of six (captain, chef, two stewardesses, deckhand, engineer). Described as blending "military heritage" with "elegant decor"; the site positions Gemaya as typically sold by individual cabin rather than as a full-ship charter.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/the-yacht-gemaya)

**UNVERIFIED:** any founding history (e.g. a claim that the company launched during the pandemic, that Elysium began sailing in 2021, or that Gemaya was added in 2023) — no history/founding page stating this was found on elixir-cruises.com. A capacity figure of "48–58 guests" for Elysium also appeared only in third-party search listings and is not confirmed against the official "Guests: 50" figure; treat the official site's own stated figure (50) as authoritative and the alternative range as unverified.

Beyond these two vessels, the site references partner-operated ships (via Yacht Cruise Company) for some products, but does not name specific partner vessels on the pages reviewed — **UNVERIFIED** which ship, if any of Elixir's own, operates the Red Sea itinerary described below.

## 2. 2026/27 deployment by region

### Greece & Aegean (Elysium)
Home port: **Lavrion**. Standard pattern across named itineraries is **8 days**. Representative examples confirmed on the official site:

- *Mythical Aegean* — Lavrion round trip calling at Delos, Mykonos, Kuşadası (Turkey), Samos, Patmos, Paros, Sifnos, returning to Lavrion. 8 days/7 nights. Listed under the site's "Specialty Cruises 2026" programme.
- *Aegean Horizon* — marketed as a "late-summer voyage," Lavrion round trip (also offered in reverse direction, "Rhodes–Lavrion") calling at Rhodes, Symi–Nisyros, Kalymnos, Lipsi–Patmos, Ikaria–Fournoi, Mykonos, Cape Sounion. 8 days.
- Other named Greek-itinerary titles referenced in site navigation (not opened in full detail): *Divine Cyclades* (described as the "signature cruise," 8 days, calling at 10 islands), *Peloponnese Ancient Paths*, *Ionian Icons* (flagged as a "limited edition 2027" sailing), and *Sporades & Cyclades* ("The Bliss Cruise"). **UNVERIFIED** in full port-by-port detail — titles and headline descriptors only were confirmed.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/mythical-aegean; https://www.elixir-cruises.com/aegean-horizon)

Season pattern implied by the "late-summer" and "2027" labelling above suggests a broadly spring-through-autumn Aegean season for Elysium, but the site does not publish a single consolidated "season dates" statement — treat overall season boundaries as **UNVERIFIED** beyond the individual itinerary labels quoted.

### Red Sea ("Kingdoms & Pharaohs")
Confirmed directly on the Red Sea page: **8 days/7 nights**, embarking at **Eilat, Israel**, then calling at **Aqaba (Jordan)**, an overnight at sea, **Hurghada (Egypt)**, an unnamed **Saudi Arabia** port, **Sharm El Sheikh (Egypt)**, before disembarking back at Eilat. Season stated on the page as **"winter sun," January–March**.
(Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/red-sea)

The page does not name which vessel operates this itinerary. Given the GSA/partner structure described above, this is plausibly a partner-operator product rather than Elysium or Gemaya — **UNVERIFIED** which ship (if any of Elixir's own) sails it.

### Specialty/theme programming (not region-specific deployment)
The site also markets recurring programme types layered onto the regional itineraries above, rather than as separate deployments: family weeks (timed to summer school holidays), theme cruises/retreats (photography, culinary, wine, creative writing, yoga, detox), honeymoons/weddings, educational school trips (with a guest archaeologist aboard Elysium), and private charters/incentive-group bookings.
(Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/elysium-journeys; https://www.elixir-cruises.com/specialty-cruises)

## 3. Positioning/clientele

- Brand tagline: **"Boutique cruising redefined"** — described on the homepage as "a completely new concept at sea."
- Stated philosophy: "a holistic approach that embraces mind, body, and soul aboard yacht-like havens of wellness and luxury."
- Product tier explicitly stated as **"the 4-star half-board concept."**
- A proprietary "Elixir Balance Concept©" is offered, letting guests select an onboard activity style from seven options: Relaxing, Active, Wellness, Fitness, Educational, Experiential, Cultural.
  (Source: Elixir official — homepage, https://www.elixir-cruises.com/)
- The underlying operator, Yacht Cruise Company, is described (on Elixir's own about-us page) as targeting "the group of travellers looking for exclusivity, intimacy, off-the-beaten-track, adventurous and special holiday settings," explicitly positioned "away from the mass market," working with vessels "from 3 up to 6-star luxury," none exceeding 300 passengers, under the tagline "The New Way of Cruising."
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/about-us)

**UNVERIFIED:** any explicit demographic or nationality/clientele statement (e.g. average guest age, home markets, GCC/Middle East guest mix) — none was found on the pages reviewed. A search-engine snippet suggested a UAE (Ras Al Khaimah) registered entity called "Elixir Cruises Maritime," but this was not stated on either official domain and should not be treated as confirmed brand history or corporate fact.

## 4. Notes for GCC agents

- **Booking/contact channel is centralised in Rotterdam**, not the Gulf: the contact page lists phone lines for the UK, Netherlands and Belgium, with booking enquiries routed to info@elixir-cruises.com / info@ycconline.com (and a WhatsApp option in the site footer). No dedicated GCC or Middle East contact point is published on the pages reviewed.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/contact)
- **The Red Sea product is the most directly Gulf/Middle East-relevant itinerary**, embarking and disembarking in Eilat with calls in Jordan, Egypt and Saudi Arabia — useful for agents pitching a regional, winter-season (January–March) short cruise, but agents should independently confirm which vessel operates it before representing it as an Elixir-owned-yacht product, since the official site itself does not name the ship.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/red-sea)
- **Fare inclusions differ by ship** (no prices reproduced, per research scope):
  - *Elysium*: cabin amenities (bathroom, climate control, TV, minibar, safe, WiFi), buffet breakfast plus half-board, welcome cocktail, barbecue, still water, unlimited coffee/tea, room service, group yoga/pilates, themed seminars, mini-gym, snorkelling/fishing/watersports gear, multilingual concierge/wellness coach.
  - *Gemaya*: similar cabin amenities plus Netflix and beach bags, buffet breakfast plus half-board, open bar, port taxes, airport/hotel transfers, water toys (sea scooter, stand-up paddleboard, kayak), and two complimentary 12-minute waterski/wakeboard sessions per cabin.
  (Source: Elixir official — https://www.elixir-cruises.com/rates-and-included)
- **No formal trade/agent portal or agent-terms page** was found on the accessible site; the rates page directs enquiries to the general contact channels rather than stating agent-specific terms. **UNVERIFIED:** whether a separate trade-facing registration or agent portal exists (a search-engine snippet suggested a registration form on the elixir.cruises domain listing "Travel Agent" as a referral-source option, but this could not be confirmed directly).
- Given the very small scale of the owned fleet (two yachts, 50 and up to roughly a dozen or so guests respectively across 25 and 5 cabins) and the GSA/partner-network structure, GCC agents should treat Elixir primarily as a **niche, high-touch boutique/yacht product for small groups, honeymoons, or bespoke charters**, and should verify current vessel, itinerary and availability details directly with Elixir/Yacht Cruise Company before quoting, since the public site does not consolidate a single master schedule.
